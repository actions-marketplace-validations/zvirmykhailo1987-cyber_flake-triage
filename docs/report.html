The trade is real: the model doubles the flakes that are not retried (4 to 8 on the holdout) to skip 174 more retries that fail again. On the small dev period it did worse (35 of 43 flakes retried), so its holdout result should be read as one measurement, not a guarantee. A team that wants fewer missed flakes can ignore the model's "do not retry" and keep the rules alone.

Limits

  • One project, 45 days. The patterns fit a Java/Spring and TypeScript/Playwright monorepo.
  • Dev is small: GitHub keeps job logs for a limited time, so most early failures have no log left.
  • Only failures someone re-ran have a label; failures nobody re-ran are probably real more often.
  • 507 holdout failures only failed because another job did; they are left out of every policy.
  • The model was run once, with one prompt, written before any model answer was seen. Its first run cut 15 of 255 answers at 1 500 tokens; those were asked again with a 3 000 limit, nothing else changed.
